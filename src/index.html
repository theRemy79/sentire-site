BEFORE YOU SETTLE IN

A few things
you might be wondering.

Simple software.
A little less uncertainty.

Is Sentire a hearing test?+

No. Sentire keeps and displays measurements from tests you’ve already had. It doesn’t test your hearing, diagnose hearing loss, or recommend treatment. Review your results and any changes with your audiologist.

Do I need an account or the internet?+

Purchase and first activation need internet. We email your license; paste it into Sentire to register this computer automatically. Afterward the app works offline. Your hearing records stay local.

Can I import an audiogram from my clinic?+

You can open a PDF, PNG, or JPG. Local import tools can suggest measurements from supported layouts, but they don’t recognize every report or symbol. Check each value against the original before saving, or enter the values manually. Keep your original report separately.

Is this a subscription?+

No. The introductory personal-license price is shown at checkout for two computer activations. There is no recurring charge.

How do I get started?+

Download the official build for your platform, purchase a license and paste the emailed license into Sentire. The app registers this computer automatically. Then enter a local name and add your first audiogram.

Can I move or share my records?+

Yes. Export a PDF report to share, or save a JSON or SQLite backup to transfer your history to another Sentire installation. You choose where the files go. Backups contain personal information, so store them somewhere private.

Can I download it from an app store yet?+

Not yet. Sentire can be distributed by direct download without an app store. Check current download availability and installation instructions on the download page.
