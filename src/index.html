BEFORE YOU SETTLE IN

A few things
you might be wondering.

Simple software.
A little less uncertainty.

Is Sentire a hearing test?+

No. Sentire keeps and displays measurements from tests you’ve already had. It doesn’t test your hearing, diagnose hearing loss, or recommend treatment. Review your results and any changes with your audiologist.

Do I need an account or the internet?+

Purchasing and first computer activation need internet. Your license is emailed after payment; paste it into Sentire to activate. No password is needed. After activation, your hearing records and license checks stay on your computer; day-to-day use works offline.

Can I import an audiogram from my clinic?+

You can open a PDF, PNG, or JPG. Local import tools can suggest measurements from supported layouts, but they don’t recognize every report or symbol. Check each value against the original before saving, or enter the values manually. Keep your original report separately.

Will I have to pay every year?+

No subscription is planned. The purchase price is $29 USD for a personal license. The current preview requires a test license; sandbox checkout does not charge money. Paid-release terms will be available before live checkout opens.

How will the license work?+

One permanent purchase includes two computer activations. Your license is emailed after purchase. Paste it into Sentire; the app registers each computer automatically. The app then verifies it offline. Recover a lost license email through the recovery page; contact support for replacement allowance if both activations are used. The local preview issues test licenses only.

Can I move or share my records?+

Yes. Export a PDF report to share, or save a JSON or SQLite backup to transfer your history to another Sentire installation. You choose where the files go. Backups contain personal information, so store them somewhere private.

Can I download it from an app store yet?+

Not yet. Linux is in local desktop testing, with Snap Store packaging planned. Windows and Microsoft Store support are a later milestone. We’ll add the official download links here when those releases are ready.
