Getting started

  1. Download the official build for your platform.
  2. Purchase Sentire using an email you can access.
  3. Paste the license from your email into Sentire and choose Activate license.
  4. Enter a local name, add your audiograms and keep a backup.

Activation needs internet once; subsequent license checks and hearing records stay on your computer. Keep your license email and order ID somewhere safe.
