PERSONAL BY NATURE

Privacy, in plain language.

Effective November 6, 2026

Seravio operates Sentire and getsentire.io. This notice covers the desktop app, website, blog, purchase/licensing service and support. Contact support@getsentire.io for privacy requests. The studio notice is at seravio.io/privacy/. Our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains hearing-related information.

Hearing records on your computer

Sentire stores the profile, hearing measurements, test details and notes you choose to save in a local database. PDF/JPG/PNG import, OCR, chart recognition and comparisons run on your computer. Imported files are sent to the app's local service on that same computer, not to getsentire.io. The app does not automatically send us your hearing records, original reports, usage analytics or crash reports, and does not send them to an AI service.

Original reports are not retained as saved-test attachments. Temporary OCR/rendering files are normally removed after processing; interruption can leave temporary files. Deleting a test removes the saved record, not the original report, exported files, recovery snapshots or other backups. Restores can create local recovery snapshots. We cannot remotely access, restore or erase files we never received.

Local databases, installation identity files and exports are not encrypted by Sentire. Protect your device, account and storage. An operating-system backup, synced folder or device administrator may copy files independently of Sentire. Uninstall is not a secure-erasure mechanism and can leave records or platform-managed backups behind. The Windows Store resubmission uses standard MSIX storage behavior: Windows may remove Store-managed hearing records, settings and activation data during uninstall or reset. Before uninstalling, export a JSON backup outside application storage and keep your license email. Hearing-history backups do not include the license or private installation identity; reinstalling can require activation again. Older portable files and separately exported backups can remain outside package-managed storage and must be managed separately. Manage local copies and backups yourself.

Information used by online services

  • Purchase: purchase email, order/license identifiers, checkout/payment references, payment status, amount, currency and discount information. Stripe collects payment details on its hosted checkout and sends the service the information needed to verify and fulfill an order. Our app and forms do not collect full card numbers or security codes.
  • Activation: the license credential, an installation public key, activation dates and support/allowance history. The public key identifies an installation, not a hardware serial-number inventory. The private installation key remains local. Initial activation and license recovery contact the service; a saved valid activation can be checked offline without a heartbeat. Request infrastructure also receives the connecting IP and headers.
  • Feedback/support: name if provided, email, platform, category, optional app version and the message you submit. The web form accepts text, not attachments or automatic app diagnostics. Email can contain whatever you attach. Please do not send audiograms, diagnoses, passwords, payment details or license tokens. If specific support needs sensitive information, we will first explain the purpose and request any additional consent required.
  • Website, blog and security: IP address, requested URL, time, browser/user-agent and other request information needed to deliver and protect the service. Our licensing/form rate limits store keyed hashes of identifiers, not raw IP addresses in feedback records. Infrastructure providers can process raw connection/security logs.

Who receives information

Cloudflare hosts the site, licensing service and its D1 database, and provides security, support-form email delivery and email routing. Stripe processes payments and related fraud/tax information. Resend sends transactional license/recovery emails. Seravio's support mailbox in Google Gmail receives support messages; replies may come from hello@seravio.io. Providers receive the information needed for their function. We do not send local audiograms to these providers for import processing.

Microsoft Store and Snap Store handle their own download/account/device information under their notices: Microsoft and Canonical. Store signing or installation does not give Seravio access to your Store account or local hearing records.

Cookies, tracking and browser signals

We do not use advertising pixels, cross-site profiling or session replay. Cloudflare injects a Web Analytics/RUM beacon for website usage and performance measurement. If loaded, it can send page/referrer URLs, a page-load identifier and browser timing metrics to Cloudflare. Cloudflare describes this as cookieless measurement without browser-storage tracking or cross-site identification; see its measurement documentation. Browser security and privacy settings can block it. The blog does not require an account, and its RSS feed is a public document; your feed reader's own behavior is separate. We serve site fonts and tutorial media from our own site.

The purchase, recovery and feedback service uses the necessary sentire_csrf security cookie, with a two-hour lifetime. It does not contain hearing history. Hosting/security providers may use essential challenge cookies; Stripe may use cookies on its own checkout. Blocking necessary cookies can prevent forms or checkout from working. We do not change behavior specifically for Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals because we do not conduct the sale, advertising sharing or cross-site profiling those choices address.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them and their copies. Online feedback records are deleted after 180 days by the service's scheduled maintenance; copies already delivered to support email are separate. Rate-limit entries expire after short security windows, up to two days in the current service, and are removed by maintenance. Successful email delivery removes the queued job; failed delivery jobs may remain for investigation and cleanup.

Correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the enquiry and related follow-up, then reviewed for deletion; an active dispute, security investigation or legal duty can require longer retention. Purchase, license, installation and audit records remain as needed to provide the perpetual license, establish ownership, prevent abuse, handle disputes and meet applicable tax/accounting obligations. Deletion can affect future activation or recovery; we explain the effect and any legally required retention before acting. Provider logs and backups follow their service retention and restoration cycles and applicable deletion obligations.

Why we process information

Where EU or UK data protection law applies, our grounds are taking steps at your request or performing a contract for product delivery and related support; legitimate interests in answering enquiries, securing services, measuring website performance, preventing abuse and maintaining necessary business records; and legal obligations for applicable tax, accounting or legal requirements. Where a feature requires consent, we request it separately. A contact request does not subscribe you to marketing. Providing form details is optional, but we cannot answer without contact information or deliver an emailed license without a purchase email.

Service providers and international processing

Cloudflare provides website hosting, security, form protection where enabled, and email delivery/routing. Google Gmail is the configured support inbox and processes messages, attachments and email metadata you send; see Google's privacy notice. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Their roles depend on the service: some act on our instructions, while others, such as app stores and payment providers, also process information for their own purposes under their notices.

Where international-transfer rules apply, a legally permitted transfer mechanism is required, such as an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about the providers and safeguards relevant to your request. We do not claim that a provider's privacy policy alone establishes compliance.

Your privacy choices and rights

Contact support@getsentire.io with a privacy request. Depending on the law that applies, you may request access, a copy, correction, deletion or portability of your information; restriction of processing; or withdrawal of consent where we rely on it. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Withdrawing consent does not undo earlier lawful processing. We do not use solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

We verify requests proportionately, normally using the email address already associated with the enquiry or purchase. Do not send identity documents or sensitive records unless we explain why they are necessary and provide an appropriate method. We respond within applicable legal deadlines and explain any lawful exception. You may ask us to review a refused request and complain to your local privacy regulator, including the UK Information Commissioner or an EU/EEA supervisory authority where applicable. We do not discriminate against people for exercising privacy rights.

Security, disclosures and external services

We use HTTPS for the public service and limit access to information needed for support and operations. No service, email account or device can be guaranteed completely secure. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or address fraud/security incidents, or in a business transfer subject to applicable privacy obligations. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Links to app stores and other websites take you to services governed by their own notices.

Children and policy changes

Our public website contact and purchasing services are intended for adults, not directed to children under 13. Do not submit children's information through public forms. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. We date changes here and provide additional notice or seek consent where required for a material change. A policy update does not authorize an unrelated new use of information already collected.
