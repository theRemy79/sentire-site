Purpose and review

Sentire by Seravio organizes measurements from hearing tests you already have. It does not test hearing, diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or replace a qualified clinician. Import suggestions and calculations can be incomplete or wrong. Check measurements against the original report and discuss hearing concerns with your clinician.
