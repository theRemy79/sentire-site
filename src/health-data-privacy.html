YOUR HEARING INFORMATION

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

This separate notice explains hearing-related information handled through Sentire, operated by Seravio. Contact support@getsentire.io to exercise your rights.

Categories and purposes

On your computer, Sentire processes the name/profile you enter, audiogram images or PDFs you select, hearing thresholds, speech scores, test dates, provider details, notes and comparisons. It uses these to import, organize, display, compare and export the hearing history you request. These app records and document-processing results are local and are not automatically sent to Seravio.

Online, a purchase, license activation, support enquiry or website visit about Sentire can reveal an interest in hearing health, even without an audiogram or diagnosis. Website performance measurement can include page/referrer URLs and browser timing metrics, as described in our general privacy notice. The service processes the contact email, product/order/license identifiers, activation installation public key and support text needed to deliver the license, activate the app, resolve your request and protect the service. If you voluntarily include health information in an email or feedback message, it reaches our support systems. Do not send medical reports or diagnoses through public support forms. We do not use visits or purchases to generate a health diagnosis or advertising profile.

Sources

Local hearing information comes from you and the report files you choose, including reports originally supplied to you by a clinic. Online information comes from you, your app's activation request, Stripe's payment confirmation and connection information generated when you request the service. We do not buy health data or pull records from medical portals or clinics.

What is shared and with whom

We do not upload local audiograms to hosting, email, payment or AI providers for import processing. Your exports and any folder/operating-system backups are under your control.

For online functions, Cloudflare processes service requests, security signals, license/activation database records, support-form email and routing; Stripe processes checkout/payment information; Resend processes transactional license and recovery email; and Google's Gmail processes support email and any content or attachment you voluntarily send. These are hosting/security/database, payment and email service providers. Seravio handles product support; no separate affiliate receives hearing records. We do not sell consumer health data, share it for targeted advertising or use geofencing to identify people seeking health care. We disclose information where legally required and subject to applicable health-data protections.

Your rights and how to use them

You choose whether to enter or import local records. Export or delete them and their local backup copies in your own storage. Seravio cannot remotely access or erase local files it never received. Declining online license activation prevents activation, but activation does not require uploading hearing records.

Where applicable law provides these rights, you may ask us to confirm whether we collect or share consumer health data, access that information and the required recipient information, withdraw consent, or delete information held by us and our service providers. Email support@getsentire.io with “Health data privacy request.” You do not need to include an audiogram, diagnosis or full license token. We verify ownership proportionately and respond within applicable deadlines. We explain any lawful exception or effect on future activation/recovery. We do not deny privacy rights merely because other records are needed for a license.

If we deny your request, reply with “Privacy appeal” for review and a written explanation. You may also contact the Washington Attorney General or your relevant privacy regulator. These rights apply according to the law; we do not require you to waive them to use support.
