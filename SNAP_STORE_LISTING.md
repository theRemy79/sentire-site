## Description

Keep your audiograms, notes and hearing-history comparisons together on your computer with Sentire.

Enter measurements, review suggestions from PDF and image imports, and compare records over time. Export PDF reports and keep local JSON or SQLite backups so you can save and restore your records.

• Organize hearing measurements and notes in one place.
• Review imported measurements before saving them.
• Compare audiograms and follow changes over time.
• Export PDF reports for your own reference or to share.
• Back up and restore your records using JSON or SQLite files.
• Keep hearing records, PDF processing and OCR on your computer.

A personal license is required: $14.95 USD introductory price, paid once, with two computer activations. Initial license activation requires an internet connection; saved activation works offline. Visit https://getsentire.io for current purchasing and download availability.

Sentire organizes hearing records. It does not test hearing, diagnose conditions or recommend treatment. Check imported suggestions against your original reports and discuss clinical questions with a qualified clinician.
